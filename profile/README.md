# Astrynn Holdings

**Engineering Advantage**

Astrynn Holdings develops and researches governed agentic AI for consequential environments, with an emphasis on institutional agency integrity, evidence, authority boundaries, coordination, and disciplined evaluation.

Our public work is organised around a simple principle:

> **Evidence over assumptions. Architecture is not implementation. Publication is not validation.**

## Research direction

### CABI 2036

**CABI 2036** is Astrynn Holdings' long-horizon research architecture and direction for studying **Institutional Agency Integrity**: whether consequential agentic systems can retain an institutionally defensible basis for action as authority, evidence, dependencies, and operating conditions change.

The public research boundary is intentionally high level.

### QEVYNOR

QEVYNOR focuses on:

- assurance;
- evidence;
- authority-related evaluation;
- Institutional Agency Integrity.

### KARVYNX

KARVYNX focuses on:

- coordination;
- dependencies;
- agent architecture;
- orchestration.

These descriptions identify research roles and questions. They do not imply that every architectural element is implemented, deployed, validated, or commercially available.

## Research discipline

Astrynn's public research is designed to preserve falsifiability and explicit uncertainty.

We do not treat:

- absence of identified prior art as proof of novelty;
- publication as validation;
- architecture as implementation;
- a single case as universal evidence;
- unsupported claims as established results.

Null results, incumbent advantage, comparison insufficiency, and negative-value or overhead findings remain admissible outcomes where the research design permits them.

## Public IP boundary

Astrynn publishes enough information to make research questions, evidence, limitations, and falsification conditions understandable.

Public material is not an unrestricted disclosure of proprietary architecture. Private primitives, internal states, decision logic, evidence models, exact handoffs, revalidation rules, schemas, gates, internal taxonomies, private prompts, credentials, and detailed evaluator mechanics are outside the public boundary unless explicitly released.
